# Last Harbor: Departure — Press copy / 宣传文案

## English

### Tagline

One harbor. One last boat. No rescue is coming.

### One sentence

A five-chapter first-person survival horror game set in a rain-soaked industrial harbor: survive infected workers and hostile security forces, restore the port's infrastructure, and find a way out before dawn.

### Short description

A port under lockdown. A technician left behind. A broadcast that never stops. Explore a stormbound industrial harbor, conserve ammunition and bring its machinery back to life in a complete five-chapter story built on Saltline's game technology.

### Store or video description

Last Harbor: Departure follows an emergency maintenance technician through the seventh night of a harbor lockdown. Cross dark cargo lanes and exposed industrial yards, survive infected workers and hostile security forces, and restore the infrastructure that might offer a way to the water. Five chapters connect briefings, dialogue and radio transmissions in a single-player story about leaving before dawn. Based on Saltline, from Felix Rieseberg's Claude Fable 5.2 experiment.

## 简体中文

### 宣传语

一座港口，最后一艘船。不会再有救援到来。

### 一句话介绍

一款设定于雨夜工业港口的五章第一人称生存恐怖游戏：躲过感染者与敌对安保力量，重启港务设施，在天亮前寻找离开的路。

### 短介绍

港口封锁，队伍失联，撤离广播却从未停止。探索暴雨中的工业港口，管理弹药，让停摆的设备重新运转。《末港：今夜离港》基于 Saltline 的游戏技术，讲述一个完整的五章逃生故事。

### 视频或商店介绍

灾变第七夜，一名失联的应急维修技术人员仍被困在紫金港。穿过漆黑货道与暴露的工业堆场，在感染者和敌对安保力量之间寻找生路，重启可能让你接近海面的港务设施。五个连续章节以报幕、人物对白与无线电展开一段天亮前离港的单人故事。本作基于 Felix Rieseberg 发起的 Claude Fable 5.2 实验游戏 Saltline 扩展与改编。
